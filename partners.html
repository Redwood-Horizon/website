For Partners

Working alongside partners to support the people we share.

Redwood Horizon provides whole-person, community-based wraparound services in Northern California. We partner with county behavioral health, health plans, community organizations, and providers to support people facing barriers to stability who would otherwise cycle through crisis systems.

Partnership Benefits

Shared Resources

Leverage our wraparound capacity and community connections to extend your service reach without expanding your infrastructure.

Coordinated Care

Reduce duplication and improve outcomes through integrated referral pathways and shared care planning.

Consistent Support

We stay with shared clients through transitions, missed appointments, and setbacks, so they don't fall through gaps between systems.

Community Presence

We connect with people through the channels already part of their lives: a phone call, a neighbor, a clinic waiting room, a tribal office, or a home visit, building support around what is easiest for each person to reach.

Who We Partner With

County Behavioral Health & Health Plans

Warm referrals, care coordination, and shared outcomes reporting for individuals enrolled in CalAIM, BHSA, and other county-funded services. We align with county priorities without duplicating existing capacity.

Community-Based Organizations

Cross-training, shared client support, and coordinated service delivery. We don't replace existing providers, we complement and extend community capacity.

Healthcare Providers

Care coordination following hospital discharge, emergency department visits, and specialty care. We ensure patients stay connected to follow-up and community-based support.

Re-entry & Justice Partners

Coordinated transition planning for people returning from incarceration, including housing, benefits, health care, and employment connections.

Making a Referral

If you're a provider, case manager, or community worker referring someone, use the Get Support page or contact us directly. We'll follow up within two business days.

For funders and foundation partners: Redwood Horizon is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, incorporated May 2026. We provide whole-person, community-based wraparound services across Northern California. For funding inquiries, contact us.

If you're interested in a partnership, MOU, or coordination agreement, reach out, we'd love to talk.
