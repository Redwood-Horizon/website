We're here. Let's figure this out together.

You don't need a referral. You don't need to have everything sorted out first.

Whether you're reaching out for yourself or someone you care about, we want to hear from you. There's no wrong way to connect.

Contact Options

  • Email: contact@redwoodhorizon.org
  • Use the form below to send us a message
  • You can also be referred by a provider, caseworker, or community organization

Referral Pathway

If you're a provider, case manager, or community worker referring someone, use the same form or contact us directly. We'll follow up within two business days.

If you need support in a language other than English, contact us and we will do our best to help.

Crisis Resources

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

Common Questions

What if I'm not sure I qualify?

That's okay — we help people figure out if our services might help them, even if they're unsure. There's no eligibility checklist. The intake conversation handles that.

Can I reach out on behalf of someone else?

Absolutely. You can reach out for a family member, client, or someone you care about. Just let us know your relationship so we can follow up appropriately.

How quickly will someone get back to me?

We aim to respond within two business days. If you need immediate support, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Information you share is kept confidential and used only to follow up with you or coordinate services with your consent.
