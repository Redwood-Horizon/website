We're Building Something New

Redwood Horizon is in its founding stage. As we grow, we will be hiring for roles that reflect our commitment to whole-person, community-based care, including care coordinators, behavioral health support staff, housing navigators, and operations roles.

We are building a team that reflects the communities we serve across Northern California. Lived experience is valued. Person-first language is standard. We work at the pace of the people we serve, including our team.

No open positions at this time. Check back or reach out if you'd like to share your interest for future opportunities.
