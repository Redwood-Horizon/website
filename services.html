What We Do

Practical support across the systems that shape stability.

Redwood Horizon provides whole-person, community-based wraparound services that help people facing barriers to stability stay connected to care and community, and move toward self-determination.

◎

Care Coordination

Connecting you to health care, following up after hospitalizations, and helping providers communicate.

♡

Behavioral Health Support

Linking you to mental health and substance use services and staying connected through treatment.

⌂

Housing Support

Finding and securing housing, preventing eviction, and navigating landlord relationships.

▣

Benefits Navigation

Helping you apply for and keep Medi-Cal, CalFresh, CalWORKs, and other supports.

↗

Re-entry Support

Coordinating services during the transition back to community after incarceration.

◇

Employment Connection

Connecting you to employment services and vocational support at the pace that works for you.

∞

Daily Stability

The practical support that holds everything together: transportation, appointments, paperwork, and follow-through.

Who We Serve

For people whose needs don't fit neatly into one program or system.

People experiencing housing instability, serious mental health conditions, substance use challenges, re-entry, transition-age instability, risk of hospitalization or institutional placement, and other complex health and social needs.

Service area: Humboldt, Mendocino, Trinity, and Del Norte counties. If you need support in another language, contact us and we will do our best to help.
