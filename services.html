Our Services

Whole-person, community-based wraparound support — tailored to what matters most to you.

What We Do

Redwood Horizon provides whole-person, community-based wraparound services that help people facing barriers to stability stay connected to care and community, and move toward self-determination. Not every person needs support in every area. We start with what matters most to you.

Care Coordination

Connecting you to health care, following up after hospitalizations, making sure your providers are communicating with each other.

Behavioral Health Support

Linking you to mental health and substance use services, and staying connected through treatment.

Housing Support

Finding and securing housing, preventing eviction, navigating landlord relationships.

Benefits Navigation

Helping you apply for and keep benefits — Medi-Cal, SSI/SSDI, CalFresh, and others.

Re-entry Support

Coordinating services during the transition back to community after incarceration.

Employment Connection

Connecting you to employment services and vocational support at the pace that works for you.

Daily Stability

The practical support that holds everything together — transportation, appointments, paperwork, follow-through.

Who We Serve

Redwood Horizon is for people navigating more than one challenge at once — people whose needs don't fit neatly into a single program or system.

  • People experiencing homelessness or housing instability
  • People living with serious mental health conditions
  • People with substance use challenges
  • People returning from incarceration
  • Transition-aged youth and young adults facing instability
  • People at risk of hospitalization or institutional placement
  • People with complex health and social needs who have struggled to get consistent care

You don't have to have everything figured out to reach out. That's what we're here for.

Service area: Northern California.

Language access: If you need support in a language other than English, contact us and we will do our best to help.

How It Works

There's no wrong way to reach out. We connect with you in whatever way is easiest for you.

Step 1

Reach Out

Call, email, or fill out the contact form. You can also be referred by a provider or caseworker.

Step 2

Connect

We'll have a conversation about what's going on and what kind of support would help. No pressure, no judgment — just listening and figuring out next steps together.

Step 3

Get Started

We build a plan together, based on your goals and what you need most right now. You set the pace — we'll be there through every step.
