About Redwood Horizon

Our mission, our approach, and the values that guide everything we do.

Our Mission

Redwood Horizon provides whole-person, community-based wraparound services that help people facing barriers to stability stay connected to care and community, and move toward self-determination.

We exist for people whose needs do not fit neatly into one program, diagnosis, funding stream, or service category — people navigating more than one challenge at once, across health, behavioral health, housing, benefits, re-entry, employment, and daily life.

Redwood Horizon stays with people through complexity.

Our vision: A California where people with complex needs are supported in community instead of cycling through homelessness, hospitalization, incarceration, and crisis systems.

Why "Redwood Horizon"? Redwoods stand rooted through fog, storm, and drought — connected underground, resilient together. The horizon is what could be: a different path, a way forward. Together they say: we are grounded in community and working toward something better.

Our Values

Six principles that guide how we show up, make decisions, and serve our community.

Whole-Person Stability

Housing matters, but housing alone does not create full stability. We support stability across multiple life domains — health, behavioral health, employment, community connection — at the pace that works for each person.

Full-Service Wraparound Support

We coordinate across systems instead of expecting people to manage fragmented services alone. No wrong door, no handoffs that leave people starting over.

Community-Based Care

Support happens where people live, receive services, build relationships, and pursue stability. We come to you — in your home, your community, or wherever is safest and most comfortable.

Locally Responsive Implementation

We adapt services to the needs, resources, geography, culture, and systems of each community served. One size does not fit all across Humboldt, Del Norte, Mendocino, Trinity, and Shasta counties.

Persistent Engagement

We stay engaged through missed appointments, housing loss, hospitalization, incarceration, relapse, and crisis. We work on each person's timeline, not ours — readiness and trust unfold at the pace that works for them.

Evidence with Humanity

We draw from evidence-based models for care coordination, behavioral health, supported employment, and re-entry, while keeping services relational, practical, and person-centered.

Our Approach

What "wraparound" means in plain language:

Most services are designed around a single problem — housing, or mental health, or employment. Wraparound is different. It starts with the whole person and coordinates across everything that affects their stability. That means housing, yes — but also health care, behavioral health, benefits, re-entry support, employment, and the daily logistics that often fall through the cracks.

How we work:

  • We come to you — services are delivered in the community, not just in offices
  • We stay with you — we don't disengage when things get complicated
  • We work at your pace — readiness and trust build on your timeline, not ours
  • We coordinate across systems — you shouldn't have to navigate fragmented services alone

Our approach draws from evidence-based models for care coordination, behavioral health support, supported employment, and working with people involved in the justice system. We adapt these models to the real conditions of Northern California — the distances, the limited resources, the communities and cultures we serve.
