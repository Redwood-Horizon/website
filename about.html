About Redwood Horizon

Built for complexity.
Rooted in community.

Our mission, our approach, and the values that guide how we show up.

Our Mission

Whole-person, community-based wraparound support.

Redwood Horizon provides whole-person, community-based wraparound services that help people facing barriers to stability stay connected to care and community, and move toward self-determination.

We exist for people whose needs do not fit neatly into one program, diagnosis, funding stream, or service category.

Our vision: A California where people with complex needs are supported in community instead of cycling through homelessness, hospitalization, incarceration, and crisis systems.

Our Values

Six principles that guide how we work.

01

Whole-Person Stability

Housing matters, but housing alone does not create full stability. We support stability across life domains.

02

Full-Service Wraparound Support

We coordinate across systems instead of expecting people to manage fragmented services alone.

03

Community-Based Care

We build support around the channels, places, and relationships already part of people's lives.

04

Locally Responsive Implementation

We adapt services to the needs, resources, geography, culture, and systems of each community.

05

Persistent Engagement

We stay engaged through missed appointments, housing loss, hospitalization, incarceration, relapse, and crisis.

06

Evidence with Humanity

We draw from evidence-based models while keeping services relational, practical, and person-centered.

Our Approach

What wraparound means in plain language.

Most services are designed around a single problem. Wraparound starts with the whole person and coordinates across everything that affects their stability.

We stay with people when things get complicated, work at their pace, and coordinate across systems so they do not have to navigate fragmented services alone.

Why “Redwood Horizon”? Redwoods stand rooted through fog, storm, and drought, connected underground and resilient together. The horizon is what could be: a different path, a way forward.
