Common Questions

Answers to the things people ask us most: about getting started, referrals, our service area, and how we work alongside you.

Do I need a referral to get support?

No. You can reach out directly, for yourself or someone you care about. Providers and caseworkers can also refer someone through the same contact form.

How do I refer a client?

Use our Get Support form or contact us directly. We accept warm referrals from providers, caseworkers, county staff, health plans, and community organizations.

What counties do you serve?

We currently serve Humboldt, Mendocino, Trinity, and Del Norte counties in Northern California. If you're outside this area, contact us and we'll do our best to help connect you to local resources.

Do you replace existing services?

No. We coordinate with existing providers and complement community capacity. Our goal is to fill gaps, not duplicate them.

How do I know if Redwood Horizon can help me?

If you're facing barriers to stability and need someone to help coordinate care, navigate systems, or just be in your corner, reach out. We'll figure out together whether we're the right fit.

What if I'm not sure I qualify?

That's okay, we help people figure out if our services might help them, even if they're unsure. There's no eligibility checklist. The intake conversation handles that.

Can I reach out on behalf of someone else?

Absolutely. You can reach out for a family member, client, or someone you care about. Just let us know your relationship so we can follow up appropriately.

What happens after I reach out?

Someone from our team will follow up to learn more about what's going on and discuss how we might be able to help. This first conversation is free and there's no commitment.

How quickly will someone get back to me?

We aim to respond within two business days. If you need immediate support, call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

How quickly do you respond to referrals?

We follow up within two business days. For urgent situations, please call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or 911.

Do you provide services in languages other than English?

Yes. If you need support in a language other than English, contact us and we will do our best to help.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Information you share is kept confidential and used only to follow up with you or coordinate services with your consent.

Can you help me apply for Medi-Cal or food benefits?

Yes. We're an authorized representative for California's benefits portal — we can help you apply for Medi-Cal, CalFresh, and CalWORKs, and follow up on existing applications. Reach out and we'll walk through the process together.

Still have a question? We're happy to talk it through.
