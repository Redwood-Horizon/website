What We Do

Redwood Horizon stays with people through complexity.

Whole-Person Support

Care coordination across health, behavioral health, housing, benefits, employment, and re-entry. We start with what matters most to you, not what fits a checkbox.

Learn More →

Community-Based Care

We connect with people through the channels already part of their lives: a phone call, a neighbor, a clinic waiting room, a tribal office, or a home visit, and build support around what is easiest for each person to reach.

Learn More →

Persistent Engagement

We stay with people through complexity, on their timeline. We don't disappear when things get hard. When one door closes, we find another way.

Learn More →

Who We're For

Redwood Horizon is for people facing barriers to stability who need practical, community-based support to move forward. Some people come to us with one urgent need, such as housing, behavioral health support, re-entry, benefits access, or connection to care. Others are navigating several systems at once and need coordinated wraparound support.

If the system hasn't worked for you, we're here to help figure out a different path. We serve adults and families across Northern California who need someone in their corner, whether they are facing one challenge or many.
