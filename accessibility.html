Accessibility Statement

Redwood Horizon is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities.

Last updated: July 2026

Redwood Horizon is committed to making our website accessible to all visitors, including those with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Accessibility Features

Our website incorporates the following accessibility features:

  • Skip navigation link — allows keyboard users to bypass repeated navigation
  • Semantic HTML structure — proper heading hierarchy and landmark regions
  • Keyboard navigation — all interactive elements are keyboard accessible
  • Visible focus indicators — clear focus outlines in Dawn Gold for keyboard users
  • Color contrast — all text meets WCAG AA contrast ratio requirements
  • Reduced motion support — respects prefers-reduced-motion system setting
  • ARIA labels — navigation and interactive elements include descriptive labels
  • Alt text — all images include appropriate alt text or are marked as decorative
  • Responsive design — site is usable across screen sizes and zoom levels up to 200%

Ongoing Efforts

We are committed to ongoing accessibility improvement. As we add new content and features, we will continue to test and refine the accessibility of our website.

Contact Us

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our site, or if you need assistance accessing any content, please contact us:

  • Email: contact@redwoodhorizon.org

We will respond within two business days and work to address your concern as quickly as possible.
